# Strange Planet — Changelog

## 2.3.1 (current)
- **All games now auto-pause when the screen turns off** or the app is backgrounded — no more losing a life because you got a notification.
- Asteroids: added sound effects for blaster fire, debris impacts, hyperspace jumps, and a thrust rumble.
- Asteroids: redesigned the UFO — warmer glow, cleaner silhouette, and pulsing rim lights that now curve along the saucer's disc instead of sitting in a flat row.

## 2.3.0
- **New: Pac-Man local 2-player Bluetooth mode.** One device plays The Being; a nearby device steers one of the four perished beings as an adversary. Pure Bluetooth — no internet required, no lag.
- The seeker assignment carries across levels, with a dedicated adversary HUD.

## 2.2.5
- Asteroids: improved UFO contrast against the cosmic background.
- Fixed a control-remap bug in the alternate button layout.
- Added captions to control buttons.

## 2.2.4
- Pac-Man: analogue joypad now fills the area below the maze and has stronger haptic feedback.
- Asteroids: added a pause button during gameplay.

## 2.2.3
- Asteroids polish: refreshed naming, larger sprites, higher-contrast bullets, improved layout, and sound effects.

## 2.2.2
- Pac-Man: a subtle haptic tick when the joystick returns to centre.

## 2.2.1
- Pac-Man: added an optional analogue joypad as an alternative to swipe controls.

## 2.2.0
- **New game: Spatial Debris Avoidance** (an Asteroids-style activity) — pilot the Rollsuck Supreme, clear the debris.

## 2.1.4
- Fixed seekers freezing in place when the player stopped moving.

## 2.1.3
- Pac-Man: remaining lives now display as avatar sprites in the score pill.

## 2.1.2
- Pac-Man: out-of-sync sock pulse animation, avatar renames, eaten-ghost dwell behaviour, and a settings text-wrap fix.

## 2.1.1
- Pac-Man: stars now fill the zones outside the maze, an opaque playfield, and a repositioned HUD pill.

## 2.1.0
- Pac-Man major update: power-up socks with a frightened/vulnerable state, a difficulty curve, lives/HUD, settings, and sound.

## 2.0.3 – 2.0.4
- **New game: Sustenance Pursuit** (a Pac-Man-style activity) with four perished-being AI personalities, waves, and a death/respawn phase.
- Visual polish: stronger being halo glow, maze floor with better contrast, HUD pill.

## 2.0 – 2.0.2
- Online Pong overhaul: WebRTC peer-to-peer transport replacing the previous backend, with lag compensation and ball-motion smoothing for fairer, smoother online play.

## 1.7 → 2.0 (highlights)
- Added the **Planetary Bulletin** launcher and per-game settings.
- **New game: Space Invaders.**
